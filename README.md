Q1) Which acquisition channel should you scale aggressively? 

Answer: Email Marketing (Top Priority) 
because it has:
Lowest average acquisition cost (~₹5.2)
Highest ROI proxy (Revenue ÷ Cost ≈ 538x) and 
Solid average revenue per customer (~₹2,826)

Strategy move

Invest more in:

Automated flows (abandoned cart, post-purchase upsell)

Segmented campaigns (high-LTV users, repeat buyers)

This channel is high-margin, low-risk scaling capital.


Q2) Which channel is the most inefficient and needs correction?

 Answer: Paid Advertising

Red flags:

Highest cost per customer (~₹30.4)

Lowest conversion rate (~1.6%)

Worst ROI proxy (~93x)

Strategic decision:

Don’t shut it down—but fix it

Narrow targeting

Shift budget to retargeting instead of cold ads

Improve landing pages and creatives


Q3) Which channel brings the best quality customers (conversion + value)?

Answer: Referral Channel

Why it matters:

Strong conversion rate (~12.3%)

Good ROI (~331x)

Healthy revenue per customer

Strategic opportunity:

Introduce:

Referral rewards

Double-sided incentives

Influencer-led referral loops

This channel compounds naturally and reduces CAC over time


Q4)Is social media worth continued investment?

Answer: Yes — but as a growth accelerator, not revenue core

Data insight:

Highest conversion rate (~16.8%)

Moderate CAC

Lower average revenue per user

Best use case

Brand discovery

New product launches

Retargeting audiences for email & referrals

Strategic positioning:

Social = demand creation
Email + Referral = demand monetization


Q5) How should you rebalance your marketing budget?

| Channel         | Current Role        | Strategic Action                     |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Email Marketing | Profit engine       | **Increase budget aggressively**     |
| Referral        | High-quality growth | **Scale with incentives**            |
| Social Media    | Awareness & intent  | **Maintain / optimize**              |
| Paid Ads        | Traffic source      | **Reduce spend, improve efficiency** |


Q6) What are the key takeaways?

Answer: 
-Growth will come from CAC reduction, not higher pricing

-Channels that feel small (email/referral) are actually strategic gold

-Paid ads should act as top-of-funnel support, not profit engine

-Ecommerce growth should be driven by email and referrals for profitability, social media for demand creation, and paid ads only as a controlled acquisition lever.
